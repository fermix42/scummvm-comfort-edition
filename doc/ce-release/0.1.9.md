# ScummVM Comfort Edition v0.1.9

Windows x64 release based on ScummVM 2026.4.0. Adds experimental detection and
clear identities for CoCo 3 OS-9 standalone and collection game variants, and
fixes the collection captions in King's Quest II and Mixed-Up Mother Goose.

The differences below describe the collection games compared with their
standalone counterparts. CE preserves these game variants and their behavior.

## Black Cauldron

Modification — Starts at Normal speed instead of Fastest, making movement
and animation slower and easier to follow.

## Gold Rush

Modification — Starts at Normal instead of Fastest. Normal speed is also
restored during another initialization/reset sequence.

Presentation — Changes how darkness is redrawn in the mine when Jerrod moves
or stops.

## King's Quest I

Modification — Defaults to Normal instead of Fastest. Leaving the title
sequence preserves the selected speed instead of forcing Fastest.

Score adjustment — The mushroom, bowl and note score adjustments stop at
their limits instead of wrapping around.

Audio — Adds sound to the water/drowning sequence. Animation pauses while
each sound plays.

## King's Quest II

Modification — Entering the first playable screen preserves the selected
speed instead of forcing Fastest.

Bug fix — Choosing Fast changes speed immediately instead of replaying the
opening theme and pausing gameplay. Choosing Slow no longer stops the music.

Presentation — The opening theme begins after a longer delay, with changes
to its repeat conditions and the pacing of the introduction.

Presentation — Moves the Space Quest promotional text one line lower and
clears the matching area of the screen.

CE bug fix — The Space Quest promotional wildlife caption displays readable
text instead of garbled characters.

## King's Quest III

Modification — Uses Normal instead of Fastest during initialization and when
control returns to the first playable area. Restart/return sequences preserve
that pace instead of forcing Fastest.

Presentation — The title sequence keeps a consistent speed instead of
switching between Normal and Fastest at different stages.

## King's Quest IV

Modification — The river/beach screen selects Normal instead of Fastest,
including when that screen initializes on a later visit.

Presentation — Clears the bottom text line during screen housekeeping,
removing messages left at the foot of the display.

## Mixed-Up Mother Goose

Modification — The bedtime opening and Mother Goose's request for help run
at Normal instead of Fastest.

Presentation — Changes how speech bubbles disappear after characters finish
speaking in several nursery-rhyme scenes.

Presentation — Changes the clearing between verses of Mary's lamb rhyme.

Presentation — Clears the name-entry area differently and moves the
personalized character-selection instruction to the right.

CE bug fix — The boy asking for pie displays his hunger/pie caption correctly
instead of showing garbled characters.

## Manhunter: New York

Modification — Removes the S-key skip from four arcade sequences, including
the electrical-grid sequence and the fights. Players must complete these
sections rather than skipping them.

Modification — Adds a cheat to the alley fight that suppresses the player's
hit reaction. During the fight, enter the character ä. On Windows, hold Alt,
type 0228 on the numeric keypad with Num Lock enabled, then release Alt.
The message "Cheat activated" confirms activation.

Timing — Signal analysis waits use elapsed time, making them less dependent
on game speed. The collection's wait is shorter at Normal speed; the
standalone's wait can be shorter at Fastest.

Audio — Adds a cue during tagged-signal analysis and changes the cue and
sound-related pause after a fight.

Presentation — Repositions instructions, map/travel prompts and the Flatbush
Bar entry prompt. Changes the clearing of password and computer-panel text.

Presentation issue — The name-lookup screen clears the upper line twice
instead of clearing both lines, which can leave the previous second line
visible.

## Police Quest I

Modification — The introduction uses Normal instead of Fast. The station's
radio/key-board hallway selects Normal instead of Fastest when initialized.

Presentation — Shows an introductory image before its accompanying sound,
so the image is visible during the cue.

Presentation — Changes how previous text is erased from the speed notice,
paperwork, wanted notices, computer search, suspect records, newspaper and
notebook pages.

Presentation — The notebook uses white lettering on a black background
instead of black lettering on white.

## Space Quest I

Modification — Starts at Normal instead of Fastest.

Presentation — Delays the opening theme, checks for a keypress before
starting it, and removes its automatic replay. Players can leave the title
screen before being held up by the music.

## Space Quest II

Modification — Starts at Normal instead of Fastest. The introduction no
longer forces Fast, and finishing or skipping it no longer forces Fastest.
The selected/default pace carries through into gameplay.

## Installation

Extract the complete ZIP to a short, writable directory. Keep the executable,
DLLs and resources together. When upgrading, preserve your complete existing
installation, including its INI, profiles, sessions and saves. The bundled
fresh scummvm.ini is for new installations. CE uses its own portable data
separately from official ScummVM. Original game data is not included.

The newly detected CoCo 3 variants remain experimental. Snatcher and Gooka
also remain experimental.
