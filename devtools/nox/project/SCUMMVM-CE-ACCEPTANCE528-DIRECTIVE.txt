## 5. No reactive debugging loops

The original development pattern remains prohibited:

Play until a blocker → fix → rebuild → replay → find another blocker → repeat.

If an earned campaign milestone fails:

1. Stop that specific acceptance path.
2. Preserve the failing state and evidence.
3. Classify the failure by its responsible native subsystem or integration boundary.
4. Investigate its general cause using source evidence.
5. Implement a general correction, not a one-off workaround for that campaign location.
6. Perform a focused deterministic regression check.
7. Resume from the nearest legitimate earned save whose state remains valid after the correction.

Do not replay the entire campaign merely because one subsystem defect was corrected.

If the correction invalidates the existing save lineage, document why and replay only the necessary legitimate progression. Do not conceal invalidated evidence.

Limit repeated attempts at the same failing check to two corrective reruns by default.

If a failure remains unresolved after that budget, record its exact blocker and evidence. Continue independent, permitted acceptance work when possible rather than looping indefinitely.

Do not introduce special-case event bypasses, fake success flags, or temporary interpreter execution to advance the campaign.

A blocking failure must not be counted as accepted progression.
