### Action and outcome invariants

1. Admission commits the chosen encounter, RNG and parent contact once. Reopening
   a prompt or loading a continuation must not reroll or advance the world clock.
2. Refusal/cancel preserves source-defined input/resource semantics. A resolved
   first hand cannot be undone by cancelling the second. Vortex invalid direction
   spends mana; ordinary pre-commit target cancellation does not.
3. An action atomically commits records, occupancy, HP/MP, status, practice,
   credit and RNG before presentation consumes its result. Preserve conditional
   draws even when protection prevents HP loss or an animation is accelerated.
4. Turn retirement selects the next source-eligible actor. Pool group is not
   synonymous with friendly affiliation. Rendering and settings do not spend turns.
5. Tactical victory awaits Escape acknowledgement; defeat never awards results.
   Quick uses its source terminal list logic and restores temporary metadata only
   on the source victory path, not after defeat.
6. Restore armor/preparation, settle XP/advancement/loot and apply authored world
   effects in source order. Retire the parent contact at its later caller boundary.
   Flee bypasses completed-contact effects. Cold restoration repeats none of them.
7. Successful integration ends with returned exploration and the next actual
   world action, or a correctly terminal defeat/recovery flow. An enemyHP or quest
   flag assertion alone is insufficient; the campaign ending requires presentation.
